EXAMPLE 1: OVERFLOWING GARBAGE DUMP IN LAJPAT NAGAR (DELHI)
================================================================================

[SCENARIO]
Ramesh, a resident of Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, is troubled because municipal
garbage has been piling up on the main road for 18 days, blocking traffic and
causing severe foul smell.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: CITIZEN INTAKE & ISSUE CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER DOES IT:
  • Opens the Streamlit App (http://localhost:8501) -> Tab 1 "Intake & Classification".
  • Types the grievance into the text box:
      "Municipal garbage dump near Central Market Lajpat Nagar has not been cleared for 18 days causing foul smell and health hazard."
  • Selects City from dropdown: "Delhi"
  • Clicks: [ 🚀 Create Case & Classify ]

WHAT THE SYSTEM COMPUTES:
  • Matched Keyword: "garbage" -> Issue Type: "solid_waste_management"
  • Municipal Authority: "MCD" (Municipal Corporation of Delhi)
  • Department: "Solid Waste Management"
  • Application Fee: ₹10 (INR) under Delhi RTI Rule 5
  • Initial Status: "ISSUE_CLASSIFIED"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: GENERATE & REVIEW RTI APPLICATION DRAFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER DOES IT:
  • Switches to Tab 2 "RTI Draft & Fee".
  • Clicks: [ ⚡ Generate / Regenerate RTI Draft ]
  • Inspects the legal breakdown and clicks: [ 📥 Download RTI Draft (.md) ]

EXAMPLE 2: CONTAMINATED WATER IN DELHI (OVERDUE -> FIRST APPEAL)
================================================================================

[SCENARIO]
Priya submitted an RTI application to the Delhi Jal Board regarding dirty,
muddy tap water in Hauz Khas on 2026-07-01. It is now 2026-08-15 (45 days later),
and the PIO has provided NO reply.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: SUBMISSION & DEADLINE CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER DOES IT:
  • Types grievance: "Contaminated brown muddy tap water supplying residential block in Hauz Khas."
  • City: "Delhi"
  • System overrides municipal body: "Delhi Jal Board (DJB)" (instead of MCD).
  • In Tab 3 "Submission & Deadlines", records actual filing date: "2026-07-01".
  • Clicks: [ 📬 Record Submission & Calculate Deadlines ]

WHAT THE SYSTEM COMPUTES:
  • Statutory Response Due Date: 2026-07-31 (exactly 30 days under Sec 7(1))
  • Overdue From: 2026-08-01
  • Status: "OVERDUE" (Alert banner: 🚨 CASE IS OVERDUE BY 15 DAYS!)
  • First Appeal Window: 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-30

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: GENERATE FIRST APPEAL UNDER SECTION 19(1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER DOES IT:
  • Switches to Tab 4 "First Appeal (Overdue)".
  • Sets Evaluation Date: 2026-08-15.
  • Clicks: [ ⚖️ Generate First Appeal ]
  • Clicks: [ 📥 Download First Appeal (.md) ]

---

### 2. Details of the Public Information Officer (PIO)

### 3. Chronology & Facts of the Case
1. **Original RTI Application Submission Date:** 2026-07-01
2. **Statutory 30-Day Response Due Date:** 2026-07-31 (under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act, 2005)
3. **Deemed Refusal Status:** As of 2026-08-15, the response is **15 days overdue**. The PIO has failed to provide any response, communication, or notice of rejection.
4. **Subject of Grievance:**
   > Contaminated brown muddy tap water supplying residential block in Hauz Khas.

---

### 4. Grounds for First Appeal
1. **Deemed Refusal (Section 7(1) Violation):** The PIO has failed to furnish the information within the statutory 30-day period mandated under Section 7(1), constituting deemed refusal of information without reasonable cause.
2. **Demand for Information Free of Cost (Section 7(6)):** Under **Section 7(6) of the RTI Act, 2005**, since the public authority failed to provide information within the statutory time limit of 30 days, the appellant is legally entitled to receive all requested information and certified records **FREE OF ANY CHARGE OR COPYING FEES**.
3. **Personal Penalty Warning (Section 20(1)):** Continued non-compliance exposes the PIO to personal monetary penalties of ₹250 per day (up to ₹25,000) under Section 20(1) upon escalation to the Information Commission.

---

### 5. Reliefs & Prayers Sought
The Appellant respectfully prays that the First Appellate Authority:
1. Direct the PIO to immediately furnish complete, certified copies of all information sought in the original RTI application dated 2026-07-01.
2. Direct that all certified records, reports, and logs be provided **free of cost** to the Appellant pursuant to Section 7(6).
3. Inquire into the cause of unexplained delay by the PIO.

EXAMPLE 3: DARK ALLEY / BROKEN STREETLIGHT IN BENGALURU
================================================================================

[SCENARIO]
Anand in Indiranagar, Bengaluru wants to report broken streetlights that make
the residential crossroad completely dark at night.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: BRIEF INTAKE & SYSTEM CLARIFICATION PROMPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER DOES IT:
  • Types initially: "Dark streetlights broken" (3 words)
  • City: "Bengaluru"
  • System instantly alerts:
      "⚠️ Description is brief (3 words). To maximize legal clarity, please specify:
       - What is the nearest landmark or pole number?
       - How long has the streetlight been non-functional?"
  • User refines text to:
      "Streetlights on 12th Main Indiranagar near 5th Cross are non-functional for past 3 weeks creating unsafe dark road."
  • Clicks: [ 🚀 Create Case & Classify ]

WHAT THE SYSTEM COMPUTES:
  • Matched Keyword: "streetlights" -> Issue Type: "streetlight"
  • Department: "Electrical / Streetlight Maintenance"
  • Municipal Body: "BBMP" (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike)
  • Application Fee: ₹10 (INR)
  • Status: "ISSUE_CLASSIFIED"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CLI RUNNER ALTERNATIVE (FOR TERMINAL USERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE USER CAN RUN IT IN TERMINAL:
  $ python scripts/cli.py create "Streetlights on 12th Main Indiranagar non-functional" --city Bengaluru
  $ python scripts/cli.py draft <CASE_ID> --output Bengaluru_Streetlight_RTI.md

WHAT THE USER RECEIVES (RTI OUTPUT QUESTIONS):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Certified copies of streetlight maintenance contracts, AMC vendor details, and inspection reports for 12th Main Indiranagar covering the last 60 days.
2. Designation and official contact details of the BBMP Assistant Executive Engineer (Electrical) responsible for maintenance of streetlights in Ward 80 (Indiranagar).
3. Certified log entries of daily power supply and bulb replacement requests for this sector.
4. Mandatory 5-day transfer under Section 6(3) if maintenance has been assigned to BESCOM or external infrastructure agency.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
